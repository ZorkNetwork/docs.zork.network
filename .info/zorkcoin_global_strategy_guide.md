## English (Global)
- **Tagline:** *“Explore Your Money.”* – our core English tagline (no translation needed).
- **Population:** ~1.5 billion total English speakers【18†L185-L193】 (global influence).
- **Crypto Trend:** *Rising.*  The U.S. remains a huge crypto market, with transaction volume up ~50% YOY in 2025【27†L192-L200】.  (Western adoption is mature; consider **holding** interest in EU overall.)
- **Fonts:** Exo 2 (supports Latin).  (No change needed.)
- **Strategy Notes:** Emphasize *exploration, clarity,* and *control*.  Western audiences value innovation but also regulatory clarity.  Highlight Zorkcoin’s usability and security. Use blue/trusted colors; avoid any suggestion of gambling.
