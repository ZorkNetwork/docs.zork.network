# Brand Strategy & Media Kit (Official Draft v1.0)

---

# 1. Core Identity

### Name

* **Zork Network** → the system
* **Zorkcoin** → the currency
* **ZORK** → ticker

---

### Tagline

> **Explore Your Money.** 

---

### Brand Essence

Zorkcoin is a simple, honest, and understandable digital currency that allows users to explore, learn, and confidently use their money. 

---

### Positioning

Zorkcoin is a straightforward digital currency designed for people who want to understand how their money works—not just use it blindly. 

---

# 2. Core Brand Attributes

Zorkcoin must consistently express:

* **Open**
* **Honest**
* **Exploring**
* **Rewarding**
* **Fresh**
* **Useful** 

---

# 3. Brand Experience Principles

### Primary Identity

> A trusted financial tool first 

Zorkcoin must feel:

* Reliable
* Predictable
* Calm
* Understandable

---

### Secondary Identity

> Exploration is optional, never required 

* Discovery elements must never interfere with core usage
* No gamification of financial actions
* No confusion for first-time users

---

# 4. Visual Identity

---

## 4.1 Logo

### Primary Symbol

* Double-struck **Z** (ℤ, Unicode U+2124)
* Must remain consistent in structure and proportion

---

### Usage Rules

**Do:**

* Use consistently across all surfaces
* Maintain clear contrast with background

**Do Not:**

* Modify structure
* Stylize beyond recognition
* Replace with alternate “Z” forms

---

## 4.2 Color System

### Primary Accent

**Zork Blue**

* `#00008B`

Usage:

* Logo
* Primary actions
* Key highlights

---

### Dark Theme (Primary Interface)

**Background Base**

* `#0B0F1A`

**Layer 1**

* `#12172A`

**Layer 2**

* `#1A2040`

---

### Supporting Blues

* `#2F4DFF` (interaction)
* `#4C6FFF` (hover/emphasis)
* `#8FA7FF` (soft highlight)

---

### Accent Color

**Electric Cyan**

* `#00D1FF`

Usage:

* Hover states
* Interactive feedback
* Subtle emphasis

---

### Neutral Colors

* `#FFFFFF`
* `#E6E6E6`
* `#999999`

---

### Color Principles

* Blue is used for **clarity and trust**, not decoration
* Avoid heavy saturation or neon styling
* Maintain high readability at all times

---

## 4.3 Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Exo 2**

* Open source
* High readability
* Supports global language expansion 

---

### Secondary Typeface

**Inter**

* UI and body text
* Clean and modern

---

### Monospace

**JetBrains Mono**

* CLI examples
* Technical documentation
* “Exploration Logs” formatting

---

### Typography Principles

* Prioritize readability
* Avoid novelty or decorative fonts
* Maintain consistent hierarchy

---

# 5. Visual Style

---

## 5.1 Design Direction

* Clean and modern
* Structured layouts
* Minimal but not sterile
* Inspired by clarity of early computing systems
  (not retro aesthetics) 

---

## 5.2 Thematic Balance

The visual system should balance:

* Subtle **depth / layered structure**
* Light **exploration cues**
* Minimal, understated reference to:

  * hidden systems
  * discovery
  * structure beneath the surface

---

## 5.3 Avoid

* Retro game visuals (pixel art, terminals)
* Neon-heavy palettes
* Overly stylized sci-fi effects
* Gamified visual language

---

# 7. Language & Tone

---

* Clear
* Calm

---

* Superiority claims
